### Getting there — Shinjuku → Gotokuji — ~15 min, ~¥200
- **Odakyu Odawara line** from Shinjuku. Odakyu has its own station building on the **west/south side** — follow *Odakyu* signs, not JR.
- ⚠️ Take a **Local or Semi-Express**. Rapid Express and Romancecar do **not** stop at Gotokuji.
- From Gotokuji Station it's ~10 min walk. (The Setagaya tram stop *Miyanosaka* is closer — 5 min — if you'd rather.)

### Getting there — Gotokuji → Shimokitazawa — 5 min
- Same Odakyu line, back **toward Shinjuku**. Shimokitazawa is 2 stops.
- The station is underground now; take the **north exit** for the main vintage streets.

### Thrifting, in priority order
1. **Second Street** and **Tanpopo House** — non-negotiable
2. **Book Off** — high
3. **Kinji**, **TreFacStyle** — if time holds

> Work **north of the tracks first**, then cross south. Most shops open 11 AM–12 PM, which is exactly why Gotokuji goes first. Give this two hours minimum.

### Getting there — Shimokitazawa → Nishi-Shinjuku — 7 min + 7 min walk
- **Odakyu**, back to Shinjuku terminus.
- Exit **west side**. Kagawa Ippuku is at Nishi-Shinjuku 7-9-15, ~7 min on foot north-west.
- Going at 1:30 PM puts you in after the office lunch rush clears.

### Getting there — Shinjuku → Shibuya — 6 min
- **JR Yamanote**, clockwise.
- **South Exit** for Sakuragaoka-cho (the nail salon) — it's the hill on the south-west side.
- **Hachiko Exit** for everything Tre is doing.

### Tre's split — MESH → Udagawacho (all walking)

**MESH.tokyo** — Miyazaki Bldg. #103, 6-28-5 Jingumae, Shibuya-ku 150-0001 · [mesh.tokyo](https://mesh.tokyo/)
Kendama and art. **Weekdays 12–6 PM · Weekends 1–7 PM.**

- From **Hachiko Exit**, walk north-east up **Cat Street** — ~10–12 min. Jingumae 6-28 is at the Shibuya end of it, so this is short.
- Coming back, cut south-west past **Miyashita Park** into Udagawacho — ~12 min. Kenscrath and Loft are both in the blocks north of the station, within sight of each other.

> ⚠️ **Go to MESH first, Loft second.** MESH shuts at **6 PM**; Loft runs to about 9. Reverse the order and you lose the kendama shop entirely — it's a small place and easy to underestimate the walk to.

> While Gia's at nails, Tre has ~2 hours, and this fills it exactly: 4:15 at MESH, out by 5:00, Udagawacho by 5:15, done in time to regroup for Sushi Labo at 8.

> **If Thursday slips:** MESH also sits right on Wednesday's Omotesando walk — Cat Street branches off the Omotesando/Meiji-dori crossing by Laforet, so it costs no extra travel. ⚠️ Not Sunday Sep 20 — it doesn't open till 1 PM at weekends and you're Ginza-bound by 1:45.
