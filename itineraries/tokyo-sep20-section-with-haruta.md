### Getting there — Akihabara → Oi-Keibajo-mae (~40 min)
- **JR Yamanote** clockwise to **Hamamatsucho** (~15 min).
- **Tokyo Monorail** to **Oi-Keibajo-mae** (~8 min). The monorail entrance is off the JR **North Exit** concourse.
- ⚠️ The monorail runs from **Hamamatsucho, not Shinagawa.** (Alternative: Keikyu to Tachiaigawa, then a 12-min walk.)
- Venue is the **Oi Racecourse** grounds, adjacent to the station.

> Go at opening. The market shuts at **2:30 PM** and sellers pack down before that — arriving at 1 PM would leave you 90 minutes of a winding-down market.

### Getting there — Oi → Omotesando (~35 min)
- **Monorail** back to **Hamamatsucho**.
- ⚠️ The Ginza line does **not** stop at Hamamatsucho. Take the **JR Yamanote** clockwise instead — to **Shibuya** (~15 min), then **Ginza line one stop** to Omotesando.
- Simplest alternative, no subway at all: stay on the Yamanote to **Harajuku** (~18 min) and walk 10 min down Omotesando into Minami-Aoyama.
- If you do take the subway, **Exit B1 or B3** puts you on the Minami-Aoyama side. yellow is at 5-10-1.

> You arrive ~45 min after opening, so expect a queue. Put your name down and walk Omotesando until they call.

### Getting there — Omotesando → Ginza (~12 min)
- **Ginza line**, one direct train, no changes. This is why the day is ordered this way.
- **Exit A13** onto Chuo-dori for Itoya.

### Getting there — Ginza → Lumine Yurakucho (7–8 min on foot)

**HARUTA · Lumine 1, 7F, 2-5-1 Yurakucho, Chiyoda-ku** · ☎️ 03-6269-9240 · **11:00–21:00**

- Walk **west along Chuo-dori** from Itoya, then cut toward Yurakucho Station. Lumine 1 is right at the station.
- ⚠️ On Sunday afternoons Chuo-dori is usually a **pedestrian zone** (*hokoten*) — you walk down the middle of the road. Pleasant, but it also means the crowds are on foot and slow.
- Stocks **both men's and ladies'** lines at this branch.
- **Sizing is true to size, in JP cm** — no need to size down. Both genuine and synthetic leather are offered, so check which you're holding.
- 💴 **Tax-free counter is on 7F, the same floor.** Bring your **passport**; the threshold is ¥5,000 and loafers clear it comfortably. Budget 10–15 min for the paperwork on top of shopping.

> ⚠️ **You're doing Loft twice** — once in Shibuya on Sep 10, again here. If HARUTA needs more than the 50 minutes above (trying on shoes plus tax-free processing eats it fast), **cut Ginza Loft**, not Itoya. Nothing is lost.

### Getting there — Yurakucho → Shibuya (~20 min)
- **JR Yamanote**, counter-clockwise, *for Shinagawa / Shibuya*. One train, no changes, ~10 stops.
- Alternative: walk 5 min back to **Ginza Station** and take the **Ginza line** direct (~16 min). Slightly faster on rails, but you give the time back on the walk — it's a wash.
- **Hachiko Exit** at Shibuya. Mo Mo Paradise is 8F of the Humax Pavilion, 20-15 Udagawacho.

> ⚠️ Leave Yurakucho by **4:25** at the latest. Mo Mo Paradise is booked for 5:00 and this leg is 20 minutes plus the walk at both ends.

### Getting there — Shibuya → Akihabara (~25 min)
- **Yamanote** counter-clockwise to Kanda, change to **Keihin-Tohoku/Chuo-Sobu** for Akihabara. Or Ginza line to Kanda.

> 💼 You fly out tomorrow with **30kg checked each**. Shoes are bulky but light — buy them today rather than trying to squeeze it into the Sep 21 morning, which is already Sensoji, Harry Cafe and a run to Narita.
