### Trains

**Ltd Exp Hashidate**, Kyoto ↔ Amanohashidate. One seat the whole way but **two railways**: JR San'in main line to Fukuchiyama, then the same train continues onto the **Kyoto Tango Railway**, a private company. That's why the fare is ~¥5,000 each way and why a JR pass wouldn't cover the last stretch.

- 🎫 **All seats reserved — no non-reserved cars.** Book both directions together.
- 💡 **It stops at Nijo**, which is 3 Tozai stops from the hotel and easier than Kyoto Station.
- 🎫 You hold **two tickets**: the **basic fare (乗車券)** for the distance and the **limited express ticket (特急券)** for the fast train and seat. **Insert both together into the gate**, take both back. Keep both until you exit.

### Buses — Tankai Bus, Ine Line (伊根線), route 5 · ¥400

- Get off at **Ine** (伊根) — not *Michinoeki Funayanosato Ine*, three minutes further and uphill.
- ⚠️ **Inewanmeguri Hide**, one stop before Ine, is the big cruise dock — the wrong end of the bay.
- **Amanohashidate Cable-shita** (天橋立ケーブル下) is the Kasamatsu stop, 26 min before the station.

### Why the sea taxi, not the cruise

1. **The cruise dock is a 30-min walk from the village.** The sea taxi leaves from Ineura Park, 2 min from the Tourist Information Center, in the village itself.
2. **On demand, 9:00–17:00** — no departure slots to build a day around.
3. **Small boat, close in among the funaya at water level.** From land you only see the backs of the houses.
4. **¥1,000 vs ¥1,200.** Minimum 2 people.

Arrange it at the **Ine Tourist Information Center** — no phone or Japanese needed. **Pay the boatman cash.**
☎️ Ine Tourism Association **0772-32-0277** — call if weather looks bad; wind or heavy rain cancels with little notice.

### Viewpoints

- **Kasamatsu Park** (north, via Cable-shita) — the *matanozoki* view. Cable car/chairlift **09:00–17:00**. Dropped today on timing.
- **Amanohashidate View Land** (south, 5 min from the station) — **Jul 16–Sep 15 term: chairlift last up 6:00 PM, last down 6:20 PM** (monorail 5:40 / 6:10). Take the chairlift: later at both ends, open-air.

### 💼 Luggage — do this first

Send the large cases to **Hotel Resol Stay Akihabara, 2-17-10 Kandasudacho, Tokyo** — where you land Sep 17. ~¥2,000–3,000 per case, next-day if lodged before the morning cutoff. Front desk handles it.

Travel to Hakone with an overnight bag only — it's ropeways, boats, small buses and ryokan stairs, the worst place in Japan to haul a suitcase. ⚠️ Tell Resol Stay Akihabara the bags are arriving ahead of check-in.

### Getting there — Kyoto → Kurama (~55 min, ~¥640)

1. **Hotel → Sanjō Keihan** (三条京阪) — 10 min on foot, or Tozai one stop
2. **Keihan main line** north to **Demachiyanagi** (出町柳) — ~5 min, ¥170. It's the terminus, you can't overshoot.
3. **Eizan line** (叡山電車) to **Kurama** — 30 min, ¥470. Platforms are **upstairs at Demachiyanagi**, above the Keihan gates.

- ⚠️ **Kurama line, not Yase-Hieizanguchi** — the line splits at Takaragaike. Check the front of the train.
- ⚠️ **Ride to Kurama (鞍馬), the last stop** — not Kibune-guchi, which is one before. You walk back over the mountain.
- Sit on the right for the maple tunnel.

### At Kurama — 1074 Kuramahonmachi, Sakyō-ku · 鞍馬寺

- **Niōmon gate** and the cedar avenue
- **Cable car** — ¥200, 2 min, Japan's shortest railway. Skips the first climb; walking it (~30 min) is nicer if the legs are willing.
- **Kurama-dera Kondō** — **¥500, reception ~09:00–16:00**. The hexagonal *kongōshō* pattern in the courtyard stone, and the valley view.

### The trail — ~2 hrs

⚠️ **Walk Kurama → Kibune, not the reverse.** Climbing is front-loaded, you descend *into* the village, and you finish among the restaurants rather than starting there.

- **Okunoin Maō-den** at the top of the pass
- **Kinone-michi** — cedar roots webbed across the path. The signature image of this walk.
- ⚠️ Cedar roots and stone steps get **genuinely slick in rain** — see the wet-weather version below.

### At Kibune — 180 Kuramakibunecho, Sakyō-ku · 貴船神社

- **Kawadoko** 🔒 — platforms built over the stream, water running under the table. Kaiseki ¥8,000–15,000 a head, reservations needed. **Hirobun** does *nagashi-sōmen* (noodles down a bamboo flume) with no reservations and a queue.
- ⚠️ **Kawadoko comes down for the year on Sep 30.** The most time-limited thing in the trip.
- **Kifune Shrine** — the vermilion lantern staircase. **06:00–20:00** through November.
- **Mizuura-mikuji** — fortunes that stay blank until floated on the shrine's spring water
- **Yui-no-yashiro** (middle) and **Okumiya** (inner), further up the valley
- ⚠️ **Kurama Onsen reportedly closed since 2021.** Don't plan on a soak.

**Back:** walk down to **Kibune-guchi** (~30 min, downhill) or local bus (¥170, 5 min) → Eizan → Demachiyanagi → Keihan → Sanjō.

### 🌧️ Wet-weather version

Skip the traverse. Straight to Kibune-guchi, up the valley, lantern steps early, long kawadoko lunch, home mid-afternoon. The gorge is mossy and atmospheric in drizzle — it loses less to rain than anywhere else here.

## 🗾 Useful Japanese

**Two tickets at the gate**
> すみません。この2枚の切符は、改札機に一緒に入れればいいですか？
> *Sumimasen. Kono nimai no kippu wa, kaisatsuki ni issho ni irereba ii desu ka?*

**Hand them over and let staff explain**
> 特急券と乗車券です。使い方を教えてください。

**Is this enough to get to X?**
> ○○まで、これで大丈夫ですか？

> 💡 Or just use the **有人改札** — the wide manned gate at the end of the row — and show both tickets.

**Shopping**
- Sunscreen — **日焼け止め** (Biore UV / ビオレUV)
- Bug-bite cream — **ムヒ** (Muhi) · category word **虫さされ**
- Pharmacy — **薬局** · medicine — **薬**
- Drugstores in Kyoto: **ダイコクドラッグ** Daikoku (open late) · **キリン堂** Kirindo · **マツモトキヨシ** MatsuKiyo

**Timetables**
- **平日** = weekdays · **休日** = Saturdays, Sundays *and* public holidays
- ★ usually means that service doesn't run on the schedule it appears in
