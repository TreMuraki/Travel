### Trains

**Ltd Exp Hashidate**, Kyoto ↔ Amanohashidate. One seat the whole way but **two railways**: JR San'in main line to Fukuchiyama, then the same train continues onto the **Kyoto Tango Railway**, a private company. That's why the fare is ~¥5,000 each way, and why a JR pass wouldn't cover the last stretch.

- 🎫 **All seats reserved — no non-reserved cars.** Book both directions together.
- 💡 **It stops at Nijo**, which is 3 Tozai stops from the hotel and easier than Kyoto Station.
- 🎫 You hold **two tickets**: the **basic fare (乗車券)** for the distance, and the **limited express ticket (特急券)** for the fast train and the seat. **Insert both together into the gate**, take both back. Keep both until you exit.

### Why the sea taxi, not the cruise

1. **The cruise dock is a 30-min walk from the village.** The sea taxi leaves from Ineura Park, 2 min from the Tourist Information Center, in the village itself.
2. **On demand, 9:00–17:00** — no departure slots to build the day around.
3. **Small boat, close in among the funaya at water level.** From land you only see the backs of the houses.
4. **¥1,000 vs ¥1,200.** Minimum 2 people.

Arrange it at the **Ine Tourist Information Center** — no phone or Japanese needed. **Pay the boatman cash.**

☎️ Ine Tourism Association **0772-32-0277** — wind or heavy rain cancels with little notice.
